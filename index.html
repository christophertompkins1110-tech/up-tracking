Parcel tracking

Follow every parcel, drop-off to doorstep.

Enter a tracking number to see exactly where a shipment is and every stop it has made.

Numbers look like UP + 9 digits + a check digit.

How a parcel moves through UP Tracking

  1. 1

    Booked

    A shipment is created at drop-off and assigned its tracking number.

  2. 2

    Scanned

    At each facility, staff record a scan — accepted, in transit, out for delivery.

  3. 3

    Tracked

    The number reads those scans back as a live route, newest first.

In this demo the shipments are pre-loaded sample data. In a live service, scans are written to a database by staff or handheld scanners and this page reads them through an API.
